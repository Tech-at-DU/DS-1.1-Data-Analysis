# Data Science Process & Tools — Solutions

Answers to the **Check Your Understanding** questions in [Lessons/DataScienceProcess.md](../Lessons/DataScienceProcess.md). Try each question yourself before reading the answer.

1. **"Clean it"** (stage 3) — handling missing values is part of data cleaning, before exploration/analysis.

2. **`.loc`** when you're selecting by label/name — column names, or index labels. **`.iloc`** is for integer-position-based selection (row 0, rows 5-10 by position) regardless of what the labels actually are.

3. Any 2 of: who or what is missing from the dataset; how was the data collected; does the sample actually represent the population you're making claims about.
